Delete Your Math Venxure Account

Math Venxure, by VClean LLC (Android app on Google Play, and the web game at mathvenxure.com)

Delete it in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Math Venxure (the Android app or mathvenxure.com) on the device you play on.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Yes, delete it.

Your account is deleted at once.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't open the game any more, email sunil@verycleanapps.com with the subject "Delete my Math Venxure account" and your game name (the name shown on the leaderboards). If you signed in with Google, send the email from that Google account's address so we can find your player. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your player and its device keys
  • Your game name (anyone can use it again)
  • Your daily puzzle results, and your places on the leaderboards
  • Your friends list, and you on other players' friends lists
  • The link to your Google account, and the record of your Pro purchase

What is kept

  • Copies in our database backups are deleted automatically within 90 days.
  • Pro stays on your Google Play account (Google keeps the purchase); it comes back if you play again and restore it.
  • Stats and settings stored on your own device stay there until you uninstall the app or clear your browser's data.
