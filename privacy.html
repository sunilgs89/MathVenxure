Privacy Policy for MathVenxure

Effective Date: February 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 2026 (version with names, leaderboards and friends)

MathVenxure ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Math Venxure app for Android (on Google Play) and the Math Venxure web game at mathvenxure.com. This page explains what information they handle and how.

By using the app or the web game, you agree to this Privacy Policy. The web game has no ads; the Android app shows ads and offers an optional Pro purchase, as described in sections 5 and 6.

1. Information We Collect

We never ask for your email address or your real name. The daily puzzles, leaderboards and friends use our game server, which stores:

  • A random player ID and a device key. Made by the server the first time you open the game. The key is kept on your device; the server stores only a one-way hash of it.
  • The name you choose for the leaderboards (required to appear on them).
  • Your daily puzzle games: your moves, taps, hints used, score, the day and the time you started and finished, and whether you played on the website or the Android app.
  • The friends you add (by their game names).
  • Sign in with Google (optional): only the account ID Google gives us for you. We don't keep your email address, name or photo.
  • Pro (Android): a one-way hash of Google Play's purchase token and the order number, to confirm the purchase with Google Play.
  • Abuse protection: when a new player is created, a salted one-way hash of the network address (IP address), only to limit how many players one network can create in a day. We don't store the IP address itself.

Advertising in the Android app is provided by Google AdMob, which collects some information itself (section 5).

2. What Stays on Your Device

  • Stats, streaks, practice games and settings are saved in the app's storage on your phone or in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data (or your browser's data for mathvenxure.com) deletes them.
  • Backup code. Settings → Backup gives you a code with your progress and your device key, to move to another device. Keep it private: anyone with it can play as you.
  • Daily reminder (Android app, optional). If you turn it on, the app asks for Android's notification permission and schedules a reminder on your phone. The schedule stays on your phone.
  • Works offline. The game's files are stored on your device. Practice works without a connection; the daily puzzles need one.

3. Leaderboards and Friends

Your game name, score, taps and hints used on each daily puzzle appear on that day's leaderboard, which every player can see (Today and Yesterday). Players who add you as a friend see you on their Friends leaderboard. Anyone can add you as a friend by your game name. Names are checked so that offensive names and names that copy ours can't be used; you can change your name twice.

The puzzles are made and checked on our server, so scores on the leaderboards are the scores the server worked out from your moves.

4. Sign in with Google (Optional)

Signing in with Google lets you keep your name, results and Pro on every device and on mathvenxure.com. Google confirms who you are to us with a signed token; we keep only the account ID in it. Google handles the sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Ads in the Android App (Google AdMob)

The free Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad when you leave a finished daily puzzle (at most once a day), a full-screen ad before some new practice boards, and optional reward videos that play only when you choose to watch one (for example, for an extra practice hint). Ads never appear while you are playing a puzzle. Pro removes the ads.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (approximate location), and information about the ads you see and interact with. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Pro and ads → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The web game shows no ads and uses no advertising services or cookies.

6. Purchases (Android App)

Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The app sends Google Play's purchase token to our server, which asks Google Play to confirm it and then switches Pro on for your player (and, if you sign in with Google, on your other devices).

7. Service Providers

  • Cloudflare runs our game server and its database (see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver it (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Google provides Google Play (downloads, purchases), AdMob (ads in the Android app) and Sign in with Google.

We don't sell your information. Sharing a result or a challenge or invite link happens through your device's own share or copy feature; the link contains the puzzle, your taps and your game name.

8. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your player and your daily results while you use the game. You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This deletes your player, name, results, friends, device keys and Pro record from the server at once (Pro itself stays on your Google Play account and comes back if you sign in again). Copies in our database backups are deleted automatically within 90 days. Without the app, see how to request deletion.

9. Data Security

All connections to our server are encrypted (HTTPS). Device keys and purchase tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

10. Children's Privacy

MathVenxure is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Your Rights

You can see your game name and results in the game, change your name (twice), and delete your account yourself (section 8). For any other privacy request, including access to the data we hold about you, contact us at the email below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
