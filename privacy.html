Privacy Policy for MathVenxure

Effective Date: February 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

MathVenxure ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Math Venxure app for Android (on Google Play) and the Math Venxure web game at mathvenxure.com. This page explains what information they handle and how.

By using the app or the web game, you agree to this Privacy Policy. The web game has no ads and nothing to buy; the Android app shows ads and offers an optional Pro purchase, as described in sections 3 and 4.

1. Information We Collect

We don't ask you to sign up, and we don't run servers that receive your game data. We do not collect your name, email address or other personal information. Advertising in the Android app is provided by Google AdMob, which collects some information itself (section 3).

2. Your Progress Stays on Your Device

  • Android app. Your game progress, scores, stats, streaks, levels, creature collection and settings are saved in the app's storage on your phone. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • Web game. The same information is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing your browser's data for mathvenxure.com deletes it.
  • Works offline. The game's files are stored on your device so puzzles can be played without a connection. These files contain the game, not personal information.

3. Ads in the Android App (Google AdMob)

The free Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: optional reward videos that play only when you choose to watch one (for example, to earn extra Magic Dust), and an occasional full-screen ad between Egg Adventure levels. Ads never interrupt a puzzle, full-screen ads never follow the daily puzzles, and Pro removes the ads between levels.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (approximate location), and information about the ads you see and interact with. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Pro and ads → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See the How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The web game shows no ads and uses no advertising services, cookies, analytics or tracking tools.

4. Purchases (Android App)

Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro is owned on your account, to switch Pro on (also after reinstalling).

5. Sharing, Challenge Links and Hosting

When you share a result or send a challenge link, the text or link contains the puzzle, your number of taps and, only if you typed one, the display name you chose. Anyone you send it to can see these. Sharing happens through your device's own share or copy feature; we don't receive it.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google Play handles app downloads and updates under the Google Privacy Policy.

If we add optional online features in the future (for example comparing scores with other players), we will update this policy before they are switched on and explain what they send and how to turn them off.

6. Earlier Versions of the Android App

Versions of the Android app before 2.0 also showed ads through Google AdMob, as described in section 3, and offered the same Pro purchase. Pro bought in an earlier version is restored automatically in version 2.0 and later.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

MathVenxure is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. User Rights

Your game data lives only on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings, or clear your browser's site data for mathvenxure.com. You may also contact us at the email below with any privacy request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
