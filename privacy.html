Privacy Policy for MathVenxure

Effective Date: February 17, 2026 · Last updated: October 31, 2026

By using the app or the web game, you agree to this Privacy Policy. Sections 1 to 4 describe the mobile app; section 5 describes the web game.

b. Usage Data

  • Game progress, scores, and interactions

c. Advertising Data

We use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob), which may collect information including:

These are used to show personalized or non-personalized ads.

We do not sell or share your personal data except:

  • With third-party services required for app functionality (e.g., ads)
  • When required by law

5. The Web Game (mathvenxure.com)

The web game works differently from the mobile app:

  • No account, no ads. You don't sign up, and the web game shows no advertisements and uses no advertising services.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Your game progress, scores, stats, streaks, levels, creature collection and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's data for mathvenxure.com deletes them.
  • No cookies and no tracking. The web game does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and does not load content from third-party services.
  • Works offline. The web game stores its own files on your device (a "service worker") so it can run without a connection. These files contain the game, not personal information.
  • Sharing and challenge links. When you share a result or send a challenge link, the text or link contains the puzzle, your number of taps and, only if you typed one, the display name you chose. Anyone you send it to can see these. Sharing happens through your device's own share or copy feature.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If we add optional online features to the web game in the future (for example comparing scores with other players), we will update this policy before they are switched on and explain what they send and how to turn them off.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

MathVenxure is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. User Rights

You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below. For the web game, you can delete everything yourself at any time by clearing your browser's site data for mathvenxure.com.
